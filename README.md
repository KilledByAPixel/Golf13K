# ☀️⛳ Sunshine Golf Classic

**Eighteen holes. Eleven clubs. One rainbow, and it is the one you leave behind you.**

A whole golf course in real 3D, in under 13 kilobytes.
Rolling fairways, water you can find, bunkers you can get stuck in,
and a forest that will absolutely stop your ball.

None of it is drawn by hand. The land, the trees, the weather, the pin and
the music are rolled out of a seed the moment you tee off, and the colours
walk from a spring meadow on hole 1 to a fall sunset on hole 18.

Finish the CLASSIC eighteen once and REMIX unlocks:
a brand new course, every single time you press it.

Your round saves itself before every shot — close the tab, come back later,
and CONTINUE puts you back exactly where you stood, mid-hole and all.

Recommended running in Chrome browser. Works on phones too.

Created by Frank Force for JS13k 2026

## 🕹️ Controls

**Mouse or touch.** Everything on screen is the interface: the chips above
the meter set club, spin and target distance, the ◀ ▶ arrows turn you,
and the meter is the swing.

- **Click or tap** = Everything. Tap the chips to change club, spin or
  distance; tap the view to look downrange at where you are aiming.
- **← → or A D** = Aim
- **↑ ↓ or W S** = Change club
- **Mouse wheel** = Target distance, 5 yards at a time
- **Space** = Swing
- **Esc** = Back to the title

## ⛳ How to Play

Aim, then swing. The swing is two clicks and only the first one is easy.

1. **Point yourself.** Turn with ◀ ▶. The ring on the ground is your target,
   and the whole meter is that distance — so half a bar is half the yards.
   It starts on the pin when you can reach it, and laid up safely down the
   fairway when you cannot, with a sensible club already in your hands.
   Change either and the ring and the meter move together.
2. **Power.** Start the meter. The cursor runs right toward the target mark,
   *bounces off it* and comes back, so every power is offered to you twice.
   Click where you want it. Let it run all the way home and the swing is off.
3. **Strike.** Now it sweeps back toward the white band at the left end.
   Hit the band and the ball goes exactly where you pointed it. Click early,
   right of the band, and you slice. Let it slip past and you hook.
   Full power *and* a pure strike paints the trail a rainbow. 🦄

Putts are one click, no strike phase, and a blue mark on the bar shows you
where the cup falls.

### 💡 Playing well

**Read the wind.** The arrow at the top right is the whole forecast: which
way it pushes, and how hard. **Nothing corrects for it but you.** It takes
the same share out of every club — about a tenth of your carry in a strong
one — so it never quietly ruins a wedge. Aim off the pin, into the wind, by
what you think it will take, then watch what it actually does. You get
seventeen more holes to use that.

**Spin is a trade between flight and roll**, and off a driver the trade is
big: backspin runs 5 yards, no spin runs 19, topspin runs 44.

- **Backspin** climbs. It lands where a normal shot lands, off a much
  higher flight, and then stops almost dead — on a green it will even suck
  back toward you. Use it to hold a green you keep running through, or to
  attack a pin with trouble behind it.
- **Topspin** flattens. It gives up about 13 yards of carry and gets more
  than twice that back on the ground. Use it to stay under a headwind, or
  to chase a long par 5 with roll you would never get through the air.
- **No spin** sits between the two, and it is the right answer more often
  than not.

**Sand is the real trap.** A bunker costs you nearly half your power on its
own — and a long club in sand digs in and throws away most of what is left.
You are handed the sand wedge automatically down there, so the mistake is
clubbing *up* out of a bunker to reach the green. Take the loft, get out,
play the next one. Rough is gentler but not free: about a third of your
power. Your lie is always named at the bottom of the screen.

**The hills are already handled.** Your target distance accounts for the
climb up to the green, so elevation is not another thing to guess — wind is
the only correction left to you.

**Putting.** The putter reaches 40 yards from anywhere, so on the green the
whole bar is 40 yards and the blue mark shows where the cup falls on it.
Putts break: the arrows on the green point downhill, and they grow longer
and hotter the steeper it gets. Where the green is flat, no arrows are
drawn at all — so bare ground is good news.

**Trees are solid.** Trunk and canopy both. There is no shot through a
forest, only around it.

### 📋 The rules

Par 3 to par 5, and the card runs all eighteen. Water or out of bounds is
one stroke and a drop at the edge. Par plus five is as bad as a hole gets:
pick it up and take the max. Your best round is kept for CLASSIC and REMIX
separately.

## 🌈 Features

- 18 procedural holes, and REMIX for a new eighteen forever
- Real 3D: hills, haze, a thousand trees, sun and shadows, all generated
- A palette that walks from spring green to a fall sunset across the round
- An 11-club bag — including a 13 iron, because js13k — plus backspin,
  topspin, and six lies that all play differently
- Wind that costs a fair share of any club, not just the small ones —
  and the lakes' waves march with it, faster the harder it blows
- A swing meter that bounces, so you get two shots at the power you wanted
- Aiming camera, chase camera, and a putt cam down at ball height
- Plays with a mouse or a touchscreen — and the aiming keys still work
  if your hands live on WASD
- Rainbow trail for a perfect strike, confetti when it drops
- Saves every shot, remembers your best round, resumes mid-hole
- Endless procedurally generated music
- ZzFX sound effects
- Built on [LittleJS](https://github.com/KilledByAPixel/LittleJS) with a custom
  WebGL renderer from [Dr1v3n Wild](https://github.com/KilledByAPixel/Drive13k)
- All written from scratch in vanilla JS
