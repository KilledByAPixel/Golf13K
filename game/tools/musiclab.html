MUSIC LAB

Plays the real updateMusic straight out of game/sfx.js — this page never copies it, so what you hear is what the game does. Edit an instrument below and hit apply to hear it immediately; edit the structure (the scale, the beat conditions, which voice plays when) in game/sfx.js and just reload this page. When you like an instrument, copy arrays puts them back in sfx.js form on the clipboard.
